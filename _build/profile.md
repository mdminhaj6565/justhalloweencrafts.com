# Brand profile — Just Halloween Crafts

Used by every article and update (checkpoint G5). Read it before writing.

## Who we are
- Site: Just Halloween Crafts (https://www.justhalloweencrafts.com), a blog of Halloween craft tutorials, costume
  accessories, free printables and party food ideas, online since 2015.
- Author line: "the Just Halloween Crafts team". Older posts keep their original author.
- Readers: parents, teachers and grown-up crafters on a budget, mostly in the US and UK, often searching in the
  weeks before Halloween.

## Voice
- Friendly, practical and calm, like a craft-loving neighbour explaining a project at the kitchen table.
- Second person ("you"), short paragraphs, plain words, American spelling, inches with centimetres in brackets.
- Lead with the answer, then the steps. Every tutorial has a supply list, numbered steps, tips and a short FAQ.
- Safety notes where they matter: hot glue, craft knives, small parts, food, candles and flames.
- Light humour is welcome; sarcasm, hype and exclamation-heavy writing are not.

## Never
- Invented statistics, studies, quotes, reviews, prices or personal stories.
- Copied text, song lyrics, long quotes from books or films, or drawings of known characters and logos.
- Keyword lists, clickbait titles, or claims of being the "best" or "#1" without proof.
- Removing the affiliate disclosure from pages with Amazon or Etsy links.
