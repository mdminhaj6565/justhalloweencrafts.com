Free Halloween Potion Bottle Labels to Print

By M. W. Johnstone. Published October 8, 2015. Updated October 11, 2026

If you want to make up a few DIY potion bottles or even an apothecary cabinet for Halloween, here's a free printable sheet of Halloween potion bottle labels that you can use.

These are all square shaped designs and you can choose to cut around the black border which is nice and easy. Alternatively cut the potion bottle labels down further and just snip out the text and the images separately.

The black print is over the top of a parchment style antiqued background which helps to age them somewhat. Enjoy the freebie and use it for your seasonal Fall crafts.

How to Use These Free Halloween Potion Bottle Labels

To use the labels, print them out onto a sheet of paper, card or sticker sheet. Cut around the black edges and then stick them onto jars, bottles and little boxes. I normally use a white liquid glue such as PVA or Elmers school glue.

Tips for Better Looking Potion Labels

Updated October 2026: printing and finishing tips added by the Just Halloween Crafts team.

  • Print on matte paper. Ordinary printer paper or matte photo paper suits the old parchment style better than glossy paper.
  • Age the edges. Rub the cut edges with a used tea bag or brush on a little cold coffee, then leave the labels to dry flat. Lightly tearing the edges instead of cutting them also makes the labels look older.
  • Stick them on smoothly. Brush a thin layer of PVA or school glue on the back of the label, press it onto a clean, dry bottle and smooth out any bubbles with a soft cloth. A coat of decoupage medium over the top protects the label from damp fingers.
  • Use a sticker sheet for speed. Printable sticker paper means no glue at all, which is handy when you are labelling a whole shelf of bottles.
