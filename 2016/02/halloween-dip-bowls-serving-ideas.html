Oranges Make Great Mini Pumpkins!

And perfect containers or little pots to hold dips and small snacks


Learn How to Make Your Own DIY Orange Pumpkin Bowl

When preparing for last year's Halloween party, I was trying to think up inventive ways in which I could serve my buffet food and especially the dips which were going to be passed around as a starter snack along with the drinks.

I had some plain dip bowls but these were very dull. What I wanted was some pumpkin style containers to go along with the large pumpkin light that a friend was making for us.

The spare oranges on the kitchen counter caught my eye, and I figured with the shape and color that they would make ideal Halloween dip pots if I treated them just like mini pumpkins. So that's what I did. You can see how it turned out and how you can make some with the link above.

If children are too young to use a knife or tool for carving, then they can use paints and stickers for decorating these little homemade pots instead.

Thanksgiving is another time where a pumpkin theme works well. The color of these oranges make them a cute way to display Thanksgiving food items too. How about surrounding the containers with golden and red leaves and Fall themed table decor.
