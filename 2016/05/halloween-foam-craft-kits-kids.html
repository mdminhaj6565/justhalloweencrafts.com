Halloween Foam Craft Kits for Kids

By M. W. Johnstone. Published May 16, 2016. Updated October 19, 2026

Quick answer: Halloween foam craft kits are boxed sets of pre-cut, often self-adhesive foam shapes that children stick together to make monsters, masks, pumpkin faces or 3D decorations. Choose a kit by the age on the box, how many projects it makes and whether the pieces are self-adhesive, or make your own from foam sheets using the ideas on this page.

In the run up to October 31st it's a great idea for adults to keep children entertained with fun crafts and activities relating to Halloween. Craft foam sheets are inexpensive to buy and come in a whole rainbow of color choices.

Halloween foam crafts are great because they are pretty cheap and they are usually pretty easy to do as well. You can buy pre-made kits like the ones shown on this page or you can use the ideas here to make up your own activities instead.

You can cut pieces into shapes for children to build up and assemble things like funny faces, costume masks, monsters and also use them to decorate pumpkins and favor or treat bags and boxes. Here's a few suggestions for sets that you can try.

What to Look for in a Halloween Foam Craft Kit

Updated October 2026: buying tips, a DIY kit idea and answers to common questions, added by the Just Halloween Crafts team.

The best kit is the one that matches the children who will use it. A few checks before you buy save a lot of frustration on the day.

  • The age label. Foam pieces, wiggly eyes and pom-poms are small, so follow the age shown on the box, and supervise younger children closely.
  • Self-adhesive or glue. Peel-and-stick pieces are quicker and less messy, which suits younger children and parties. Kits that need glue give older children more freedom to design their own monsters.
  • Number of projects. For a class or a party, a multi-pack where every child makes the same item is easier to run than one large model.
  • Finished size and use. Magnets, masks and door hangers are used afterwards; a 3D haunted house is a longer project better suited to older children or a family afternoon.

How to Make Your Own Halloween Foam Craft Kit

You can put together a homemade kit with a pack of craft foam sheets, a few templates and some extras. Cut out simple shapes in advance (bats, pumpkins, ghosts, tombstones, monster eyes and mouths), then pack each set in a zip bag with wiggly eyes, glue dots and a pipe cleaner or two.

  1. Print templates from our free printable Halloween crafts collection and trace them onto foam with a ballpoint pen.
  2. Cut the shapes with sharp scissors; adults can use a craft knife for small inside details.
  3. Bag one set per child with a picture of the finished idea so they know what to aim for.
  4. Add a magnet strip, a lolly stick or an elastic band so the finished piece becomes a fridge magnet, a puppet or a mask.
Tip: Foam masks are a natural next step. Our paper plate Halloween masks use the same cut-and-stick approach, and foam pieces stick to them well with glue dots.

What age are Halloween foam craft kits for?

Most foam kits are aimed at children of school age, but the right age depends on the kit, so check the label on the box. Peel-and-stick kits with large pieces are easiest for younger children; kits with lots of small parts or 3D assembly suit older children, ideally with an adult nearby.

What glue works best on craft foam?

Glue dots and double-sided foam tape work instantly and are clean for children to use. Tacky craft glue also holds well but needs drying time, so lay pieces flat while it sets. A low-temperature glue gun is strong too, but should be handled by an adult because the glue gets hot.

Are foam craft kits good for a Halloween party?

Yes. They need no paint, little setup and are easy to tidy away, which makes them a good choice for a party craft table. Give each child the same small project, such as a magnet or a mask, and keep it short for younger children so everyone finishes before attention runs out.
