Luna Lovegood Quotes from the Harry Potter Books

By M. W. Johnstone. Published March 2, 2016. Updated October 11, 2026

This post contains affiliate links. If you buy through them, the site may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Disclosure.

This sayings, Luna Lovegood quotes and sentiments page presents my favorite quips all from the lovable and very eccentric Harry Potter witch herself. The main thing I love about this character is her fashion sense because it really rocks!

You may not know but the very first mention of the Lovegoods is written long before they ever appear into Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, which is book 4 in the series. This mentions that "the Lovegoods have been there for a week already" and it relates to the Quidditch World Cup which is on at the time.

Luna who has a very unfortunate nickname "Loony" appears in the Harry Potter books 5 through to 7. Despite the odd nickname, she became a very strong and inspiring member of Dumbledore's Army.

About Luna's Spectrespecs (Spectra Specs)

Funnily enough, the thing that most people seem to remember about Luna are her amazing Spectrespecs glasses. In the movie, Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince, it is the Spectrespecs which enable her to discover Harry who is hurt and invisible.

However, that scene never even happens in the original book because it was actually Tonks who found Harry. Of course, anybody who's seen Harry Potter will know immediately who you're dressing up as if you wear these funky glasses!

How to Use Luna Lovegood Quotes in Halloween Crafts

Updated October 2026: craft ideas added by the Just Halloween Crafts team.

Luna's gentle, slightly odd way of seeing the world makes her quotes lovely for friendly Halloween decorations that aren't too scary. Some ideas:

  • A Ravenclaw banner. "Wit beyond measure is man's greatest treasure" looks great printed in bronze on blue card and strung across a bookcase. Use printable banner letters for a bigger version.
  • A party welcome sign. "Don't worry. You're just as sane as I am" is a perfect, funny greeting for guests at a costume party.
  • A Nargles tag. Tie a small paper tag with Luna's mistletoe and Nargles line to a sprig of greenery on the door.
  • Costume details. If you're dressing up as Luna, a butterbeer cork necklace and radish earrings are easy, recognizable touches to make at home.

Quote crafts are best kept for personal use and parties, as the books' text is copyrighted. You'll find more ideas in our Halloween quotes and sayings and fantasy crafts collections.
